Ground rules

Dating is personal. So are the rules.

Six rules shape every introduction, so the person in front of you is real, considered, and there for the same reason you are.

  • ID verification Verified member

    Every member is ID verified.

    Anyone can make a profile on a swipe app. Here, every member is ID verified.

  • One introduction at a time.

    No crowd to compare against and no juggling chats. One person gets your full attention.

  • You · YesThem · Yes

    Nothing moves without both of you.

    You see a preview of their profile first. It only becomes a conversation if you both say yes.

  • Call

    A no-texting policy.

    Texting is where conversations lose momentum and fade away. Here, it’s a call first, and video when you’re ready.

  • Human judgement+AI

    Human judgement in every introduction.

    Not just AI. Your psychology, attachment patterns, values and lifestyle are considered before anyone is introduced.

  • Tell us how it felt.

    If it doesn’t click, we listen.

    Talk it through if you want to. The next introduction is shaped by it.

Who it’s for

Let’s be honest about who this is for.

We don’t want more crowd. We want the right crowd.

This is for you if

  • You want to find a life partner, but don’t want to be rushed into getting married just because “it’s time”.
  • You are willing to put in the real time and effort the process requires, rather than simply dating to pass time.
  • You want to date with the intention of marriage, and believe in monogamy.
  • You are open to genuinely giving this process an honest chance.

This isn’t for you if

  • You are looking to keep things casual and see where they go.
  • You tend to ghost people, because you like having multiple options and conversations going at the same time.
  • You are looking for validation, instant chemistry, or something to pass the time.
  • You would rather browse through profiles than take the time to genuinely get to know a person.

Right nowWe serve a small crowd: 26 to 34 year olds, heterosexual cis men and women, never married. We hope to open this up to many more people soon.
